Meet the Artist

JuniperzCreations is an independent New York City art brand creating miniature sculptural art, wearable pieces and original paintings.

Each piece is created with careful attention to storytelling, texture and detail. The work celebrates nostalgia, culture, creativity and the small things that bring people joy.
